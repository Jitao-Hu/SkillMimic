## Weekly Report (Last 7 Days)

**Time Range**: 2026-03-06 to 2026-03-12  
**Project**: SkillMimic – Dual Humanoid HRL

---

### 1. Algorithm & Training Improvements

- **HRL hyperparameter tuning for dual humanoid (multi-commit)**  
  - Adjusted key hyperparameters including `entropy_coef`, `learning_rate`, `imit_reward`, `horizon_len`, and minibatch size to improve stability and sample efficiency.  
  - Introduced a capped penalty to reduce unproductive trajectories and stabilize training dynamics.  
  - Increased maximum episode length and refined training frequency settings to better match the dual humanoid control requirements.

- **HRL agent logic extensions**  
  - Expanded `hrl_dual_agent` with additional logic for **skill monitoring and logging**, enabling per-skill behavior inspection during training.  
  - Added a substantial amount of new code to improve observability, debugging, and analysis of HRL behavior at the skill level.

---

### 2. Environment & Task Logic Updates

- **Dual task enhancements (`skillmimic_dual`)**  
  - Significantly extended the dual humanoid task implementation with 200+ new lines of code.  
  - Refined the task flow, reset logic, and several edge-case behaviors to better support complex interaction tasks (e.g., pass and catch).  
  - Added **per-attempt statistics** to track success rates and performance at the attempt level, enabling more fine-grained evaluation of task difficulty and policy quality.

- **Reward, observation, and control adjustments**  
  - Reworked the **catch reward** design to better align incentives with task success and to reduce reward hacking opportunities.  
  - Updated the observation space configuration and control frequency for the dual humanoid HRL task to improve learning dynamics and policy responsiveness.  
  - Tuned termination conditions in both task and environment code, making episode endings more consistent with task semantics.
